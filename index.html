Gallic Acid Photo Test

GA with 0.5% FeCl3 · v

Research prototype. Room-light accuracy is still being validated. Do not use results for food-safety, medical or legal decisions.
  1. Place one drop of the sample on clear plastic film over white paper.
  2. Add one drop of 0.5% FeCl3 on top of it.
  3. Wait 10 to 15 seconds.
  4. Take a photo from directly above, flash off, with film visible all around the drop and no shadow on it.
  5. Tap the centre of the drop, then its edge.

Step 1 of 2: tap the centre of the drop.

    Measurement details

    What this app does

    Gallic acid reacts with iron(III) to form a blue-black iron-gallate complex. The app compares the colour of the reacted drop with the clear film around it. It uses four colour ratios (red, green, blue and grey) and the CIEDE2000 colour difference, and a random-forest model gives the gallic acid level. No grey card or other colour reference is needed.

    Privacy

    Your photo is analysed inside your browser. It is never uploaded or stored. The app works offline after the first visit.

    How good is it?

    The model was trained on 63 videos (3 phones, 7 levels from 10 to 10,000 mg/L, 0.5% FeCl3, LED lamp). We tested it on a phone and reaction it had not seen, using a single video frame:

    • Correct level band in 79% of tests; every error was off by one band only.
    • Typical error of the numeric estimate: within a factor of about 1.8 (median); 80% of results within a factor of about 4.
    • First detectable level about 100 mg/L. At 1000 mg/L and above the colour saturates.

    Not yet validated: photos under ordinary room light, different droppers and other phones. A validation study is under way, and this page will be updated with its results.
